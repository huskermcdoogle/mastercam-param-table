    Hit a target time

    Ribbon: Parameter Table tab › Improve › Hit a target time

    What this is for

    You say how long some ops should take. The tool finds one factor for their feeds that gets there – each feed kept inside its own limits – and shows every op before and after: time, flips and MRR. Nothing is written until you click Apply.

    When to use it

    • A quote or a schedule needs the part (or a tool's ops) under a certain time.
    • “Take 10% off this tool's time” – and you want to see what that does to the inserts first.

    Steps

    1. Select the ops (any cell in their rows), or click a cell in one op of a tool.
    2. Click Hit a target time. The window opens beside the sheet.
    3. Ops: Selected rows, or Every op of tool ... (the tool of the row you are on).
    4. Target: type the time you want for those ops, like 12:30 (minutes:seconds) or 1:02:00 (hours:minutes:seconds) – or a change: -10% for 10% less time, +10% for 10% more. Always type the sign: a bare 10% is refused with say which way.
    5. Options: Main cutting feed only is ticked to start (plunge, retract and back feeds stay as they are). Tick Speeds too to change speeds by the same amount.
    6. Press Enter or click Preview. Read each op: time, flips and MRR before and after, and the feed change. Enter only previews – it never writes.
    7. Click Apply to write it. Ctrl+Z takes it all back.

    Only the cutting part of the time can change. Rapids, tool changes and dwells stay the same, so a very short target may not be reachable. The window says how close it gets.

    Speeds too: the flips column then gets a * and a note. Flips are counted from cut time, so a faster speed looks like fewer flips – but a faster speed wears the edge sooner. Check the edge time with Inspection & inserts.

    Worked example

    The bore ops 7 and 8 take 1:59:24 together. The cell lead wants 10% off.

    1. Select a cell in op 7's row and op 8's row. Click Hit a target time.
    2. Type -10%, press Enter. The main feeds go up 11.2%.
    3. Read the preview, then click Apply.
    Opfeedtime beforetime after
    7 Rough Bore ID0.01 → 0.011121:44:001:33:35
    8 Finish Bore ID0.01 → 0.011120:15:240:13:51
    Both1:59:241:47:26 (-10.0%)

    Flips per part stay at 11 for the tool. Then check the insert: is the new feed inside the maker's range for that insert and that depth? If not, the time is a wish, not a plan.

    What the messages mean

    Press Enter (or Preview) to see what this changes, op by op.
    Type the target, then press Enter or click Preview.
    '10%' - say which way: +10% for more, -10% for less.
    Type the sign. -10% means 10% less time.
    ... the target 1:47:28 is out of reach
    The feeds could not get exactly there (limits, or time that is not cutting). The line says how close it got.
    2 cell(s) held at a limit: ...
    Those feeds could not go as far as the others. The other ops make up the difference where they can.
    Not applied yet - Apply, or Revert to follow the sheet again.
    A target is typed. The window keeps it while you click around the sheet to check.
    The sheet changed since this was worked out - here it is again. Check it, then Apply.
    Something changed the cells. The plan was worked out again.
