    Hit a target time

    Ribbon: Parameter Table tab › Improve › Hit a target time

    What this is for

    You say how long some ops should take. The tool finds one factor for their feeds that gets there – each feed kept inside its own limits – and shows every op before and after: time, flips and MRR. Nothing is written until you click Apply.

    When to use it

    • A quote or a schedule needs the part (or a tool's ops) under a certain time.
    • “Take 10% off this tool's time” – and you want to see what that does to the inserts first.

    Steps

    1. Select the ops (any cell in their rows), or click a cell in one op of a tool.
    2. Click Hit a target time.
    3. Operations: pick the selected ops, or every op of the tool.
    4. Target: type the time you want for them, like 12:30 (minutes:seconds) or 1:02:00 (hours:minutes:seconds) – or a percent, like -10% for 10% less time.
    5. Options: tick Speeds too to change speeds by the same factor (the time then goes down with feed × speed). Tick Main cutting feed only to leave plunge, retract and back feeds alone.
    6. Press Enter or click Preview. Read each op: time, flips and MRR before and after, and any cell that hit its limit.
    7. Click Apply. Ctrl+Z takes it all back.

    Only the cutting part of the time can change. Rapids, tool changes and dwells stay the same, so a very short target may not be reachable. The window says how close it gets.

    Worked example

    The bore ops 7 and 8 take 1:59:24 together. The cell lead wants them under 1:40:00.

    1. Select a cell in op 7's row and op 8's row. Click Hit a target time.
    2. Type 1:40:00, press Enter. The feeds go up about 20%.

    7 Rough Bore ID0.010 → 0.0121:44:00about 1:27:00follows the shorter cut time

    Then check the insert: is 0.012 per rev inside the maker's range for that insert and that depth? If not, the time is a wish, not a plan.

    What the messages mean

    Press Enter to work out what this changes.
    Type the target, then press Enter or click Preview.
    A cell marked as at its limit
    That feed could not go as far as the others (its op's limit). The other ops make up the difference where they can.
    Select operations (cells in their rows) on the 'Lathe params' sheet first.
    Go to the main sheet and select cells in the ops' rows.
